FIELD GUIDE

How to play this prototype

1. Choose a power. The seven power tabs show their supply-center and unit counts. At the start, the official 1901 units are deployed.

2. Give orders. Select a unit and choose Hold, Move, Convoy move (army), Convoy (sea fleet), Support hold, or Support move. Alternatively, click a unit on the map and then a highlighted province to order an ordinary move. Issue convoy moves from the order desk. Repeat for the other powers. You can leave units unassigned: they hold.

3. Resolve. Press the large button to process every country's orders simultaneously. The after-action report shows outcomes. If any units are dislodged, enter retreats; otherwise the game advances to the next phase.

4. Continue. Fall supply-center capture and winter builds/disbands are implemented. Sea fleets can convoy armies to coastal provinces, including over multisea chains. A dislodged fleet breaks its convoy route unless another valid route survives.

5. Keep your game. Autosave stores the position on this device. Export a JSON backup to preserve it or import one later.

Switzerland is impassable. Rome and Venice share a regular land border; armies can move directly between them. After each winter, non-supply-center land adopts the last occupant’s color unless adjacent to land shaded or owned by another power. This color has no rule effect. Convoys need an army order to move by convoy and matching orders from every fleet in a sea-route chain. The NC / SC / EC badges mark separate fleet-access coasts of St. Petersburg, Spain and Bulgaria. Province labels are abbreviated on the map. Hover or tap to select a province and read its full name in the order editor.
