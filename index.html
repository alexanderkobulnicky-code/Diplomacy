FIELD GUIDE

How to play this prototype

1. Choose a power. The seven power tabs show their supply-center and unit counts. At the start, the official 1901 units are deployed.

2. Give orders. Select a unit and choose Hold, Move, Support hold, or Support move. Alternatively, click a unit on the map and then a highlighted adjacent province to order a move. Repeat for the other powers. You can leave units unassigned: they hold.

3. Resolve. Press the large button to process every country's orders simultaneously. The after-action report shows outcomes. If any units are dislodged, enter retreats; otherwise the game advances to the next phase.

4. Continue. Fall supply-center capture and winter builds/disbands are implemented. Convoy orders are not yet available, so this is an early prototype rather than a complete game.

5. Keep your game. Autosave stores the position on this device. Export a JSON backup to preserve it or import one later.

Province labels are abbreviated on the map. Hover or tap to select a province and read its full name in the order editor.
